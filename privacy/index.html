2. Wofür die App da ist

Elite Dent ist eine Fachanwendung für Zahnarztpraxen und Dentallabore (iPad). Sie ist kein Patientenportal und kein diagnostisches Medizinprodukt. Verarbeitet werden nur Daten, die für den Labor-Fallablauf nötig sind: Akten, Scans, Farbe, Form, Fotos, Chat, Export.

4. Zwecke

Ausschließlich der Betrieb des zahntechnischen Fallablaufs: Anmeldung, Akten, Qualitätsprüfung, Farb- und Formarbeit, Austausch Praxis↔Labor, DATEV/XML-Export, Offline-Nutzung am Stuhl. Keine Marketingprofile, keine Drittanbieter-Werbung.

2. What the app is

Elite Dent is a professional app for dental clinics and labs (iPad). It is not a patient-facing product and not a diagnostic medical device. We process data only as needed for the lab case workflow: records, scans, shade, shape, photos, chat, and export.

3. What we collect

This matches the app’s privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) and the features actually used. Purpose: app functionality, not advertising. No tracking, no ads, no sale of data (NSPrivacyTracking is false).

4. Why

Only to run the dental-lab case workflow: sign-in, records, quality checks, shade and shape work, clinic↔lab communication, DATEV/XML export, and chairside offline use. No marketing profiles, no third-party ads.

5. Legal bases and roles

Account data for dentists and lab staff: typically GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (contract to use the app) and, where applicable, Art. 6(1)(f) (keeping the service secure).

Health and patient data (Art. 9): the clinic enters this data in the course of care. Elite Dent hosts it so the app can run. In practice:

  • the clinic is controller of the patient record,
  • Elite Dent is processor (Art. 28) for hosting and operating the app,
  • joint controllership (Art. 26) may apply to some steps — to be set out in the data-processing agreement.

The clinic DPA and retention policy are not yet finalized: [DPA / RETENTION — PLACEHOLDER]. Until then, do not put real patient data in non-EU or demo-only environments.

6. Where data lives and how it is protected

  • Location: EU hosting is intended. Concrete region: [HOSTING / SUBPROCESSORS — PLACEHOLDER]. EU-region Postgres and object storage are not yet marked complete on the internal checklist.
  • In transit: HTTPS/TLS in production. (Production TLS is still an open checklist item — once live, TLS applies.)
  • On device: the app encrypts the local offline cache (queued photos/scans) on the iPad. Settings state that patient data is encrypted on device and in transit to the EU API.
  • At rest on the server: AES-256 at rest for scans/photos/XML is documented and being completed; it is not internally checked off as done. We do not claim it as finished here.

In-app access is role-scoped (clinic staff see their patients; the lab sees the case workflow). Activity logging on sensitive actions is partially in place and is being extended.

7. Retention

Account data: for the life of the service contract plus legal duties. Patient and case data: as instructed by the clinic and as required by applicable German professional and commercial retention rules. Exact periods: [DPA / RETENTION — PLACEHOLDER].

  • Auth / password reset: the password-reset web page uses Supabase for authentication. Region, DPA, and subprocessor list: [HOSTING / SUBPROCESSORS — PLACEHOLDER].
  • API / file hosting: servers and storage for cases, media, and export — provider and location: [HOSTING / SUBPROCESSORS — PLACEHOLDER].

No sale of data. No advertising networks.
