Send scans and photos to your iPad

Files go to your private inbox. You add them to a patient on the iPad.

  1. Sign in with the same account you use on the iPad.
  2. Drop scans (.ply, .stl, .obj) or photos (.jpg, .png, .heic, .webp, .tif), up to 300 MB each.
  3. On the iPad open Incoming: scans on the Scans tab, photos on the Camera tab.
