Send scans and photos to your iPad

  1. Sign in with the same account you use on the iPad.
  2. Drop scans (.ply, .stl, .obj) or photos (.jpg, .png, .heic, .webp, .tif), up to 300 MB each.
  3. On the iPad, open Incoming: scans on the Scans tab, photos on the Camera tab. Check the file, then add it to the selected patient.

Files wait privately for you for up to 30 days, then are deleted if not added to a patient. Closing or reloading this page signs you out.
